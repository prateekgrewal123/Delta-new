Why, Who & Where to Travel by Air

Learn how travelers can think about air travel, choose destinations and plan routes without using a flight-comparison or booking tool.

✈

Why travel by flight?

For long distances, air travel can reduce travel time and connect travelers with destinations that may be difficult to reach by ground transportation. The right choice depends on trip distance, schedule, budget and personal preferences.

◎

Who is air travel for?

Air travel can suit business travelers, families, solo travelers, students, vacationers and people visiting friends or relatives. Consider luggage, mobility needs, connection time and the total airport-to-destination journey.

⌖

Where should you travel?

Choose a destination based on the purpose of the trip, season, airport access, travel time, and the number of connections required.
