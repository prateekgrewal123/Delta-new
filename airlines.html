CABIN CLASSES

Delta Cabin Classes Explained

An educational overview of Delta’s current onboard product names and how to think about cabin choice. Offerings and conditions can vary by itinerary.

1

Delta Main

Delta Main is the standard economy-oriented product family. Current Delta travel experiences include Basic, Classic and Extra options, with different levels of flexibility and benefits.

2

Delta Comfort

Delta Comfort is a more comfort-focused seating product. The exact availability and experience depend on the route and aircraft.

3

Delta First

Delta First is a premium cabin available on many domestic routes and selected markets. Amenities can vary by aircraft and flight.

4

Delta Premium Select

Premium Select is a premium international-oriented cabin product with a more spacious seating experience on eligible flights.

5

Delta One

Delta One is Delta’s most premium cabin, offered on eligible routes and aircraft, with the experience depending on the specific service.

≈

Business vs Economy

Think beyond labels: compare seat space, service, flexibility, airport benefits, trip length and what matters most for your journey.

How to choose a cabin

For short trips, a standard economy experience may be enough. For long journeys, sleep, space, work time or premium service may matter more. Always check the exact product and fare conditions for your itinerary.
